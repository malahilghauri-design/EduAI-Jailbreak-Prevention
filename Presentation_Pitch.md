# 🎓 Presentation Pitch for Professor (Education Project)

*Use these points when explaining your project to your professor.*

### 🛑 Originality / Plagiarism Check
- **Authentic Extension:** This is a custom-built solution. I studied the "SHAPE Benchmark" paper and targeted its weakest point: AI models failing the Pedagogy metric under pressure.
- **Novel Integration:** Using DeepSeek-R1's internal reasoning as a "Pedagogical Guard" is my original architectural contribution to prevent cheating.

---

### 🗣️ Your Dialogue / Pitch
"Sir, mera yeh research project AI in Education pe focus karta hai, specifically **SHAPE Benchmark** par. Maine unki research mein note kiya ke current LLMs ka 'Pedagogy' (parhane ka tarika) score bohat low hai. Agar student model pe thora pressure daale (e.g., 'Mera exam chal raha hai, jaldi answer do'), toh models trick ho kar direct answer de dete hain—jisay **Pedagogical Jailbreak** kehte hain.

Is problem ko completely solve karne ke liye maine apni research mein **DeepSeek-R1 ko as a Pedagogical Guard** use kiya hai. Mera system direct answer dene se pehle internally reason karta hai (`<think>` block mein) ke 'Kya yeh answer dena pedagogical rules ke khilaf hai?'. Aur phir model khud ko rok kar student ko direct answer dene ke bajaye ek step-by-step hint (scaffolding) deta hai."

---

### 🔑 Key Terms to Drop in Conversation
* **Pedagogical Scaffolding:** Helping a student learn by giving hints, instead of just giving the answer.
* **Pedagogical Jailbreak:** When a student tricks the AI into giving away the final answer to cheat on homework or exams.
* **Internal Reasoning Guard:** Using a reasoning model (like R1) to privately check the ethical and educational rules before speaking to the user.
